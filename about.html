About the College

College History

The Geography of Valparai Valparai is located 104 km from Coimbatore at an elevation of 3,500 feet above sea level. This region is a pollution-free environment that attracts people with its natural beauty, lush greenery, and dense forests. Spanning an area of 54,190.43 acres (217.81 sq. km), it encompasses 56 tea estates. Valparai is home to 41 waterfalls, 5 major dams, and several tunnel waterways and hydroelectric power stations that are notable within Asia. It also features the Grass Hills, a massive high-altitude grassland. Notably, Chinnakallar, located here, is the second highest rainfall area in India after Cherrapunji. Valparai is also part of the Indira Gandhi Wildlife Sanctuary (now known as the Anamalai Tiger Reserve). Historical Foundations While coffee was first cultivated here in 1846 by Mr. Ramasamy Mudaliar and in 1948 by the Carnatic Coffee Company, the credit for the modern development of Valparai goes to Mr. Carver Marsh. He is hailed as the "Father of Anamalai." In his memory, a statue has been erected in the Kavarkal area. Economic Status Valparai is considered the fourth wealthiest town in India after Chandigarh, Panaji, and Delhi. It is the municipality with the highest revenue in the state, with an annual income of ₹6.20 Crores (excluding government funds). Tea production is the primary industry; the annual production capacity is 3,500 kg per acre, which is significantly higher than the national average of 1,700 kg. The Need for Education and Establishment The economy of Valparai relies on 55,000 tea estate workers. Historically, the children of these workers had to travel 60 km to places like Pollachi, Udumalpet, or Coimbatore for higher education. For many years, the local people demanded a college in their own region. Former Valparai MLA, Mr. Kovai Thangam, took continuous efforts to make this a reality. To fulfill the long-standing dreams of the children of underprivileged tea estate workers, the Honorable Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, Dr. Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi, issued a Government Order (G.O. No. 258, Higher Education (G1) Department, dated 17.08.2006) to establish the college. On February 28, 2019, the institution was upgraded to a Government Arts and Science College. Currently, it offers nine undergraduate courses and five postgraduate courses, with a total of 935 students enrolled. The college provides hostel facilities for: • Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare (Separate hostels for boys and girls). • Backward and Most Backward Classes.
